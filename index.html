Multi-Layer Testing Strategy

We're testing on both Polygon L3 and Arbitrum Orbit L3 to determine the best mainnet deployment

🔬 Our Testing Approach

Rather than rushing to mainnet, we're conducting comprehensive testing on both layer architectures. This data-driven approach ensures we launch on the most suitable infrastructure for payment use cases.

Why Two Testnets? Each layer has unique advantages. By testing both simultaneously, we can make an informed decision based on real performance data, not assumptions.

🟣 Polygon L3 (Amoy)

  • ✓ Large developer ecosystem
  • ✓ Proven zkEVM technology
  • ✓ Strong community support
  • ✓ Established partnerships
  • ⏳ Testing gas efficiency
  • ⏳ Measuring transaction finality

🔵 Arbitrum Orbit L3

  • ✓ Ultra-low gas fees
  • ✓ High transaction throughput
  • ✓ Optimistic rollup stability
  • ✓ Customizable parameters
  • ⏳ Benchmarking performance
  • ⏳ Stress testing capacity

2.2 Issues We're Addressing

  • High Transaction Fees: Credit card processors charge 2-4% + fixed fees
  • Slow Settlements: Traditional payments take 1-3 days to settle
  • Layer 1 Gas Costs: Ethereum mainnet transactions too expensive for small payments

We're competing against giants with billions in funding and millions of merchants. Our advantage is lower fees and crypto-native infrastructure, but merchant adoption is our biggest challenge.

3. Our Solution

3.1 KBTPay Payment Gateway

Our core product is a merchant payment gateway that:

  • Accepts ETH, USDT, USDC, and other ERC-20 tokens
  • Charges 0.5% transaction fee (vs 2-4% traditional)
  • Settles in minutes instead of days
  • Runs on Layer 3 for low gas costs ($0.001-0.01 per transaction)
  • Provides fiat conversion options (future feature)

3.2 Technical Components

Payment Flow:

  1. Customer selects crypto payment at checkout
  2. KBTPay generates payment address
  3. Customer sends payment (monitored on-chain)
  4. Smart contract holds funds in escrow
  5. After confirmation, merchant receives funds (minus 0.5% fee)
  6. Escrow released, transaction complete

  • Standard: ERC-20
  • Supply: 1,500,000,000 (fixed, no minting)
  • Decimals: 18
  • Testnet Address: 0x2753...6D81 (Sepolia)

Presale Contract

  • Accepted Currencies: ETH, USDT, USDC
  • Price Tiers: Dynamic pricing based on stages
  • Vesting: Locked until mainnet launch

  • Function: Holds payments until confirmation
  • Timeout: 24-hour automatic refund if unconfirmed
  • Fee Structure: 0.5% to protocol treasury
